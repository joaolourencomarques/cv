João Lourenço Marques is Associate Professor with Habilitation at the Department of Social, Political and Territorial Sciences of the University of Aveiro, lecturing courses in the areas of Urban and Regional Planning and Public Policy (quantitative methods and techniques to support decision making).

He received his PhD degree in Social Sciences from the University of Aveiro (European PhD), after the MSc in Innovation and Regional Development Policies, and his graduation studies in Urban and Regional Planning by the same university. He was visiting researcher at the Universities of St Andrews, Dundee and Heriot-Watt (UK).

He is an integrated member of the Research Unit in Governance, Competitiveness and Public Policies (GOVCOPP), coordinating the Group of Systems for Decision Support. In the Research Group on Planning and Innovation (GETIN-UA) he has been conducting and coordinating research projects in strategic spatial planning, decision support systems, spatial competitiveness analysis, demographic dynamics and forecasting, and econometric and economic models for regional development.

He is director of the Master in Data Science for Social Sciences and Head of the Department of Social, Political and Territorial Sciences. He was also a member of the boards of the Portuguese Association of Urban Planners (APU) and of the Portuguese Regional Science Association (APDR).

Research interests

The development of tools and methodologies to support decision-making across the phases of policy making: description (what is happening), diagnosis (why is it happening), prediction (what is going to happen) and prescription (what needs to be done). Applications cover housing, education, health, demography, mobility, infrastructure and employment, at territorial levels from local to national.

  • Decision support tools in planning and public policy
  • Socioeconomic and territorial dynamics
  • Demographic forecasts
  • Local and regional planning strategies in housing and education

Domains of specialization: data science for social sciences · spatial analysis methodologies · foresight and forecasting · data analysis techniques.
